# Agency North Star

**Working product direction.** This describes the experience Agency is being built toward.
It is not a list of features already available in the package.

Make it easy for people and organizations to build trusted working relationships,
do excellent work together, and get paid with clarity and dignity.

Agency should help everyone answer four questions: What have we agreed to? What happens
next, and who owes the next action? What may I see or do? How does this work become an
accepted outcome and a fulfilled obligation?

## Four connected journeys

| Journey | Intended outcome |
| --- | --- |
| Win | A client's need becomes an understood, agreed engagement. |
| Staff | Willing, suitable people can become ready and accept work with clear terms. |
| Prove | Participants understand progress, review evidence and make acceptance decisions. |
| Settle | Work and financial obligations are reconciled with visible payment and dispute status. |

These journeys can overlap and repeat. They are not a compulsory funnel or fixed module map.

## Relationships continue between projects

A vetted consultant should be able to remain part of the agency network without a current
assignment. People can offer availability, consider work, pause and return. Availability
is distinct from a commitment, and membership is distinct from access to an engagement.

Clients, partners, employees and consultants need useful profiles and shared work context
without being pushed into a public social feed. Visibility is deliberate and limited to
the appropriate audience.

## Fit the tools people already use

Adoption should start with a useful connection to an existing workflow. Email, text
messages, calendars, professional networks such as LinkedIn and X, and existing execution
tools are candidate integration surfaces. Provider availability and permitted capabilities
must be verified for each implementation.

Preserve conversation context, link to the relevant action, and return a clear receipt.
Support deep links, imports and notifications before requiring a complete system migration.
Use a secure application step when identity, sensitive documents, authorization or an
explicit agreement require it.

Agency should maintain authoritative business state across channels without copying whole
mailboxes or treating an incoming message as permission to act. Users need clear account
linking, sharing choices, delivery preferences, disconnect behavior and a usable fallback
when a provider is unavailable.

## Principles

- Make expectations and unresolved questions visible before commitment.
- Show the agency's obligations and waiting time alongside the participant's tasks.
- Collect only what the stage requires, with an understandable purpose and audience.
- Keep credentials, clearance verification, readiness and access decisions distinct.
- Use work evidence to support decisions without monitoring people's lives.
- Show payment states honestly: submitted, reviewed, disputed, scheduled, initiated and confirmed.
- Let governed agents reduce coordination work while retaining accountable human decisions.
- Design withdrawal, corrections, pauses, returns and departure as complete experiences.

## How to judge progress

The proposed central measure is reliably fulfilled work commitments: agreed outcomes
accepted and their due payment obligations confirmed fulfilled. Its precise measurement
contract remains open.

Balance that measure with time waiting on agency decisions, onboarding clarity, freshness
of capacity information, disputes, overdue obligations, privacy and coordination effort.
Measure failures on the agency's side as deliberately as participant completion.

For adoption, examine time to a first useful outcome, repeated data entry, connection
failures and work that can be completed in a user's existing flow. Connection counts
alone do not establish usefulness.

## Focus

Start with one complete, respectful relationship journey, then connect readiness,
assignment, evidence and payment. Reuse specialist tools and independent components.
Defer a feature when its contribution to informed commitment, appropriate readiness,
accepted work or fulfilled obligations cannot be explained.
